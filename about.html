About Us

Supplying and installing precast well rings, water, wastage and septic tanks with a focus on durability, safety and timely service across Chikkamagaluru region.

Well Ring & Tank Installation Services

Manjunatha Well Ring Works specializes in the design, casting and installation of precast concrete well rings, water tanks, wastage (wastewater) tanks and septic tanks for domestic, agricultural and institutional applications. Our products are engineered for long service life, accurate sizing and dependable performance in varying soil and groundwater conditions.

Every unit is produced using controlled batching, specified reinforcement and disciplined curing for structural strength, dimensional stability and leak resistance. We guide clients on diameter, wall thickness and depth configuration—then manage excavation, placement, ring alignment, joint sealing and backfilling. Customized solutions address site constraints, drainage and future maintenance access.

Our commitment includes quality materials, safety at installation, timely delivery and reduced waste through efficient casting practices. Clients receive end-to-end support—from technical sizing to post-installation care—backed by local expertise and reliability.

Our Process

  1. Assessment: Soil, water table & access review.
  2. Sizing: Diameter, thickness & depth recommendation.
  3. Casting: Controlled mix, reinforcement & curing.
  4. Excavation & Base Prep: Stable foundation formation.
  5. Placement & Alignment: Sequential ring lowering & vertical check.
  6. Joint Sealing: Leak prevention & structural continuity.
  7. Backfilling & Finish: Compaction and surface readiness.
